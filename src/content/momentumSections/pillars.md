The "What Your Gift Powers" cards. Their titles, copy, icons, and links are
edited in **Momentum Fund gift pillars**, not here — on the admin dashboard that
collection sits under /donate, alongside this one.

This entry decides where the band sits on the page and, via its Heading field,
what the band is called. Blank Heading keeps the shared "What Your Gift Powers"
title, which lives in `src/data/donatePage.json` as `pillarsTitle` and needs a
developer to change; filling Heading in here renames the band yourself, and is
what tells two pillars bands apart.

Which cards this band shows is the Card group box here paired with the Group box
on each pillar — type the same word in both. Blank on both sides shows the
ungrouped cards, which is how the band reads today, so leave Card group alone
unless you are splitting the band in two.
