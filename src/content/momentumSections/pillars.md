The "what your gift powers" cards. Their titles, copy, and links are edited in
**Momentum Fund gift pillars**, not here — on the admin dashboard that collection
sits under /donate, alongside this one.

The cards are numbered 01, 02, 03 from their position in this band, so reordering
them in the pillars collection renumbers them automatically. There is nothing to
set: the old Icon box is gone, because the numbers replaced the icons it chose
between.

This entry decides where the band sits on the page and, via its Heading and
Kicker fields, what the band is called. Blank Heading keeps the shared "What Your
Gift Powers" title, which lives in `src/data/donatePage.json` as `pillarsTitle`
and needs a developer to change; filling Heading in here renames the band
yourself, and is what tells two pillars bands apart.

Which cards this band shows is the Card group box here paired with the Group box
on each pillar — type the same word in both. Blank on both sides shows the
ungrouped cards, which is how the band reads today, so leave Card group alone
unless you are splitting the band in two. A second band numbers its own cards
from 01 again rather than continuing this one's count.
