The donor recognition wall. The names come from the Donors collection, and the
heading, intro, giving levels, and the "no donors yet" message are edited under
Settings → Momentum Fund page copy — this entry only decides where the wall sits
on the page. It sits after the member quotes and before the closing ask, so a
reader meets the people already behind the fund immediately before being asked to
join them. Move it with the Order field or hide it with the Draft toggle.
