Behind every event, chapter, and member of our network is an opportunity for a conversation, introduction, or shared experience that has helped Harvard alumni in tech move forward.

> Sometimes, the most meaningful opportunities begin with a single conversation.

An introduction to someone who has been where you are going. A roundtable where a new idea becomes sharper. A community gathering that turns a shared interest into a lasting collaboration.

A moment of encouragement that reminds someone they are not building alone.

Harvard Alumni in Tech exists to create more of these moments, the kind that help alumni find one another, support one another, and move further together.

Across cities, industries, and career stages, our network brings together founders, builders, operators, students, investors, and innovators who believe in the power of community.

Because when exceptional people are connected to the right community, one conversation can open a door, one relationship can shape a path, and one network can help create what comes next.
