The numbered priorities list that sits directly under The Momentum Network. It is
the same band as "What your gift powers" — the `pillars` kind — in a different
arrangement, so everything true of that band is true here.

Two fields make it its own thing rather than a copy of the other one:

- Group is `priorities`, so it draws only the gift pillars tagged with that
  same group and leaves the untagged ones to the band above. Change it and this
  band silently empties.
- Layout is `list`, which stacks the entries as full-width rows divided by
  hairlines with the number in its own column, instead of the three-across
  bordered grid. Any other value — or a blank one — falls back to that grid.

Edit the entries themselves in **Momentum Fund gift pillars**; they are the ones
whose Group reads `priorities`. They number 01, 02, 03 from their position in
THIS band, so the count restarts here rather than continuing from the band above.

Each entry can carry an Amount — what that line costs — printed under its
description in crimson. It is free text rather than a number on purpose: these
figures are ranges and annual costs as often as exact one-off sums ("$8–12k a
year"), and a number field would force a currency and a formatter onto what is
really editorial copy. An entry with no Amount simply prints none; the grid
layout ignores the field entirely.

Move the whole band with Order, or hide it with the Draft toggle.
