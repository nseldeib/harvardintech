The "what we've accomplished so far" band. The figures beneath the heading are
entries in **Momentum Fund accomplishments**, not page copy — on the admin
dashboard that collection sits under /donate, alongside this one.

This entry decides where the band sits on the page and, via its Heading and
Kicker fields, what the band is called. Blank Heading falls back to
`accomplishmentsTitle` in `src/data/donatePage.json`, which is not editable in
the CMS and needs a developer to change; filling Heading in here renames the band
yourself, and is what tells two accomplishments bands apart. Blank Kicker simply
draws no small line above the heading.

Which figures this band shows is the Card group box here paired with the Group
box on each figure — type the same word in both. Blank on both sides shows the
ungrouped cards, which is how the band reads today.
