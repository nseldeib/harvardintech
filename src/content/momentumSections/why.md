We are a volunteer-led alumni community that has grown into a global network of founders, technologists, operators, investors, and professionals who are building, mentoring, launching, and opening doors for one another.

To meet this moment, we need the infrastructure to support the momentum we are already seeing. That means more consistent programming, wider access to meaningful connections, stronger tools and resources for the volunteers who run it, and the operational foundation needed to serve our community at a higher level.

Your support is what gives Harvard Alumni in Tech the ability to plan ahead rather than event to event, to invest more intentionally, and to create a stronger, more sustainable alumni-powered ecosystem.

Now is the time to move from informal support to intentional investment. Together, we can create an impact that lasts beyond any one event, message, or moment.

> The future of this network will not be built by chance. It will be built by alumni who believe in what this community can become and choose to help power it forward.
