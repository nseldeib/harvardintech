- Create Instagram posts and Stories featuring Harvard Alumni in Tech events, including event highlights, speakers, key takeaways, and post-event moments.
- Develop content spotlighting Harvard alumni doing extraordinary work across technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and leadership.
- Create posts and Stories featuring Harvard faculty, their research, insights, accomplishments, and contributions to the technology community.
- Turn event photos, videos, quotes, and recordings into engaging Instagram content.
- Develop recurring Instagram content series that highlight the people, ideas, and impact within the Harvard Alumni in Tech community.
- Create timely content around relevant technology trends, alumni news, and community milestones.
- Help identify creative ways to increase engagement and strengthen Harvard Alumni in Tech’s presence on Instagram.

Requirements

- Minimum of 3 years of experience working in social media marketing, content creation, or a related area.
- A portfolio of social media work is required.
- Proficiency in Canva for creating branded social media graphics and content.
- Experience using Instagram’s built-in video editing tools to create and edit Reels and other short-form video content.
- Strong understanding of Instagram content formats, including posts, Stories, Reels, and carousels.
- Ability to create engaging, polished content that aligns with an established brand and audience.
- Strong attention to detail and ability to work independently within project guidelines.
