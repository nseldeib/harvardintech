blurb: |
  Help grow Harvard Alumni in Tech’s visibility and engagement on LinkedIn by actively engaging with relevant Harvard University-related content. This project focuses on identifying appropriate posts from Harvard schools, programs, faculty, alumni, and affiliated organizations, then thoughtfully liking and commenting to strengthen HIT’s presence across the broader Harvard community.

**Requirements**

- At least 2 years of experience using LinkedIn for professional networking, community engagement, social media, or digital marketing.
- Strong understanding of LinkedIn etiquette, tone, and professional communication.
- Ability to identify relevant content from Harvard schools, programs, faculty, alumni, student organizations, and affiliated groups.
- Strong writing skills and the ability to leave thoughtful, relevant comments that add value to conversations.
- Good judgment when engaging publicly on behalf of Harvard Alumni in Tech.
- Familiarity with technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, or the broader Harvard community is a plus.
- Ability to work independently and consistently follow an established engagement strategy.
