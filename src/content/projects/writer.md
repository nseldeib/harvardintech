title: Writer
blurb: |
  Help bring timely and relevant technology stories to the Harvard Alumni in Tech community by researching and writing engaging articles on emerging technologies, innovation, industry trends, and the people shaping the future of tech. Content may feature expert perspectives, notable developments, and topics of interest to Harvard alumni working across the technology ecosystem.
---

**Requirements**
- Minimum of 5 years of professional writing experience, with demonstrated experience covering technology-related topics.
- A portfolio or published writing samples are required.
- Strong research, interviewing, and fact-checking skills.
- Ability to translate complex technology topics into clear, engaging, accessible articles.
- Strong understanding of technology, innovation, emerging trends, and the broader tech ecosystem.
- Excellent writing, editing, grammar, and storytelling skills.
- Ability to work independently, meet deadlines, and follow editorial guidelines.
