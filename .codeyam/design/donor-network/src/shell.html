Part one · built

The wall as it stands

Open it live ↗

This is on the Momentum Fund page today. It is organised entirely by giving level, which is the assumption every direction below either keeps or throws away — so it is worth deciding what you want to keep before choosing a design.

  1. W1
    Giving levels are the structure. Supporters are grouped into bands by level, each with its own heading and a line about what that level funds. Anyone whose level is blank or unrecognised collects in a trailing Other supporters band rather than disappearing.
  2. W2
    Founding Donor is a badge, not a level. It cuts across the bands, so a founding donor keeps whichever level actually reflects their gift instead of being pushed into one that misstates it.
  3. W3
    Anonymity withholds four things at once. Name, photo, outbound link, and even the initials — two letters beside “Anonymous supporter” identifies someone to anyone who knows them. The real name stays in the entry for your records. This is the one thing on the page that a later edit cannot take back, which is why it is built in rather than handled by convention.
  4. W4
    Filter chips, and a note that opens. One chip per band, plus All; a card reveals the supporter's note when clicked. Both are conveniences — every name is in the page itself, so with JavaScript off the wall is the same complete list it always was. The chips only appear when there is more than one band to move between.
  5. W5
    Empty is the designed default, not a gap. There is no supporter list yet, so this is what the page shows today and will keep showing until the first import: an invitation and the giving button, rather than an empty scaffold. Whatever replaces the wall inherits this requirement.

Part three · not built

The spreadsheet import

Quote these as I1–I4

However the recognition page ends up looking, supporters have to get onto it from the bi-weekly spreadsheet. This is a walkthrough of that, not working software — click through it and see where it stops. It stops in the same place every time, and that place is the reason this section exists.

Open questions these directions can't answer on their own
  1. Q1
    What is it called? Nicole ruled out “wall” but left five candidate names. Each direction carries a different one so they can be judged in place rather than on a list.
  2. Q2
    Is $500 the founding threshold, and does the spreadsheet say so? The note asks it as a question. Since the upload carries no dollar amounts, founding status has to arrive as its own column — or be derived from when someone gave. These mockups assume the early cohort. See I3.
  3. Q3
    What happens to a supporter who asks not to be named? The live site already withholds an anonymous donor's name, photo, link and initials together (W3). Search and a shareable card are both new ways to undo that, so here an anonymous supporter is a visible node that counts toward every milestone but cannot be looked up or turned into a card. Worth confirming that is the behaviour you want.
  4. Q4
    Are school and graduation year public? They are the only structure the spreadsheet offers, and directions 01, 03 and 09 are built on them — but they are also personal details some supporters may not expect to be listed. They are not currently fields on the site at all.
