Open questions these directions can't answer on their own
  1. What is it called? Nicole ruled out “wall” but left five candidate names. Each direction carries a different one so they can be judged in place rather than on a list.
  2. Is $500 the founding threshold, and does the spreadsheet say so? The note asks it as a question. Since the upload carries no dollar amounts, founding status has to arrive as its own column — or be derived from when someone gave. These mockups assume the early cohort.
  3. What happens to a supporter who asks not to be named? The live site already withholds an anonymous donor's name, photo, link and initials together. Search and a shareable card are both new ways to undo that, so here an anonymous supporter is a visible node that counts toward every milestone but cannot be looked up or turned into a card. Worth confirming that is the behaviour Nicole wants.
  4. Are school and graduation year public? They are the only structure the spreadsheet offers, and directions 01, 03 and 09 are built on them — but they are also personal details some supporters may not expect to be listed.
